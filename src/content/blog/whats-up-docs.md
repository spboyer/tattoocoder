I put out a survey on twitter a few months ago on "What was challenging you most about ASP.NET Core"

> What is challenging you most about [#aspnetcore](https://twitter.com/hashtag/aspnetcore?src=hash)
> 
> — Shayne Boyer (@spboyer) [April 26, 2016](https://twitter.com/spboyer/status/725026985085308928)

...ironically it was **[Documentation](https://github.com/aspnet/docs)**.

Looking over the list of issues there is a range of topics to cover. Everything from simple variable renaming to full tutorials.

Now I am asking you, the community, what do you want to have in the docs, what's most important to you?

[ASP.NET](https://github.com/aspnet) is open source, and the [Docs](https://github.com/aspnet/docs) are included in that umbrella. So create issues, +1 to get them prioritized, comment, and share.
